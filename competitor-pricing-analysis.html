How to read confidence. Figures marked ✅ Verified passed independent 3-vote adversarial checks against the vendor's own page plus aggregators (G2, Capterra, SaaSworthy, GetApp). 1.The market splits into two pricing models

A · Subscription / SaaS

Flat fee per month / year / event, sometimes + attendee add-ons.

Zoho Backstage, Eventify, Swapcard, Accelevents, Whova, Brella, Cvent, Bizzabo, RingCentral/Hopin

B · Transaction fee (pay-per-ticket)

% + fixed fee per paid ticket. Free events are free.

Townscript, Explara, KonfHub (India/GCC) — and globally, Eventbrite

🎯 The single most important finding for EventHex: onsite badge printing + check-in hardware is almost always gated behind a quote-only enterprise tier (Swapcard, Whova, Brella, Cvent). Eventify is the rare exception that publishes it. That is EventHex's clearest positioning gap — a transparently-priced, mid-market, all-in-one plan that includes onsite badging/check-in.

5.Recommended pricing structure for EventHex

EventHex is an all-in-one platform with strong India/GCC presence competing against Western SaaS incumbents. The positioning gap: transparent, mid-market pricing that bundles onsite badging/check-in — which competitors hide behind quote-only enterprise tiers — while offering the transaction-fee flexibility the India/GCC market expects.

TierTargetSuggested priceIncludes
FreeTrial / small free events$01 event, ~100 registrations, registration + basic ticketing, mobile app, basic analytics. Free events always free.
StarterSmall paid / SMB$99–149/eventor ~2% + fixed per paid ticket. ~250 attendees, microsite, ticketing, check-in app, badge designer, coupons.
ProfessionalMid-market conferences$299–399/event or ~$249/mo~1,000 attendees, onsite badge printing + hardware check-in, networking/1-1 meetings, agenda, gamification, exhibitor/lead capture, full analytics.
Business / White-LabelAgencies, franchises$799–1,199/event1,500+ attendees, white-label + custom domain, branded app, AI copilot, API, priority support, multi-event portfolio.
Enterprise5,000+ / GCC govt & corporateQuoteUnlimited scale, SLAs, dedicated onsite hardware, custom development, SSO.

Why this positioning wins

  • Onsite badging at Professional, not enterprise — undercuts Swapcard/Whova/Brella/Cvent (all quote-only); matches only Eventify, which starts higher and per-venue.
  • Both per-event and transaction-fee billing — India/GCC organizers (used to "free events free, pay per ticket") aren't forced into a Western subscription. ~2% + fixed undercuts Eventbrite (3.7%+$1.79) and Townscript (2.99%+₹10).
  • Entry between the two worlds — above the India fee floor, well below Western SaaS: Professional ~$299/event vs Zoho $599 / Eventify $599.
  • Region-specific pricing (India/GCC in INR/AED, West in USD) — every incumbent is USD-flat; localized + free-events-free is a concrete regional edge.
  • Transparent published pricing as a brand differentiator — most enterprise rivals (Cvent, Bizzabo, RainFocus, Whova, Brella) are quote-only, which mid-market buyers dislike.

6.Known gaps & open questions

  • No verified pricing for Cvent, Bizzabo, RingCentral/Hopin, Hubilo, Airmeet, Splash, RainFocus, Dreamcast — enterprise players are known quote-only but figures are indicative only. A targeted follow-up or direct sales-quote requests are needed.
  • Geographic variance (US vs UK/EU vs India/GCC) firmly captured only for Townscript (IN vs Intl) and Eventbrite (US). Whether Zoho/Eventify/Swapcard/Accelevents localize for India/GCC is unconfirmed.
  • Refuted claims (do not use): Explara paid-tier scaling, Explara white-label $0.99/registration + 4% gateway, KonfHub one-time $499/$1499 plans.
  • Exact per-tier subscription $ for Accelevents' Professional/Business/Enterprise were not captured.
  • Every vendor page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch; verification relied on search-index snapshots + aggregators. Confirm any number before publishing externally.

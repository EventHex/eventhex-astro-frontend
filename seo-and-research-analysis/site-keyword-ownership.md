## Centralized-dashboard ownership decision

- Primary theme: centralized control of multiple events.
- Best-fit clusters: centralized event management dashboard, multi-event management dashboard, event portfolio dashboard.
- Supporting mechanisms: portfolio attention signals, unique-people visibility, returning attendees, selective event cloning, event-level access, cross-event comparison, CSV export, and separate event workspaces/sites.
- Demoted broad terms: event management software and event management platform.
- Reason: the broad terms describe the complete EventHex product and are better owned by the homepage or feature hub.

# All-in-One Event-Tech — Competitor Pricing Analysis

**Purpose:** Inform EventHex's own Plans & Pricing decision.
**Date compiled:** 2026-07-15
**Method:** Multi-source deep research (fan-out web search → source fetch → 3-vote adversarial verification → synthesis). 25 pricing claims verified; 22 confirmed, 3 refuted.

> **How to read confidence:** Figures marked **✅ Verified** passed independent 3-vote adversarial checks against the vendor's own page plus aggregators (G2, Capterra, SaaSworthy, GetApp). Figures marked **⚠️ Indicative** come from search-index snapshots that did *not* survive the full verification pass — treat them as directional, confirm before quoting externally. Nearly every vendor pricing page blocks direct fetch (HTTP 403), so even verified numbers rest on cached snapshots + aggregators, not live page reads. **All prices are a 2026 snapshot and drift over time.**

---

## 1. The market splits into two pricing models

| Model | How you pay | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Subscription / SaaS** | Flat fee per month / year / event (sometimes + attendee add-ons) | Western mid-market & enterprise: Zoho Backstage, Eventify, Swapcard, Accelevents, Whova, Brella, Cvent, Bizzabo, RingCentral/Hopin |
| **B. Transaction fee (pay-per-ticket)** | % + fixed fee per **paid** ticket; **free events are free** | India/GCC regional: Townscript, Explara, KonfHub — and globally, Eventbrite |

Several vendors **blend both** (e.g. Accelevents: transaction fee on free/starter tiers, flat subscription with *zero* platform fee on paid tiers; Whova: quote-only subscription **and** a per-paid-ticket fee).

**The single most important strategic finding for EventHex:** onsite **badge printing + check-in hardware** is almost always gated behind a **quote-only enterprise tier** (Swapcard, Whova, Brella, Cvent) — Eventify is the rare exception that publishes it. That is EventHex's clearest positioning gap: *a transparently-priced, mid-market, all-in-one plan that includes onsite badging/check-in.*

"All-in-one" here = **registration + ticketing + badges/check-in + engagement/networking + analytics** in one platform.

## 3. Detailed competitor breakdown

### A) Public-pricing SaaS (mid-market)

#### Zoho Backstage ✅ Verified
Two billing models on the same tiers, plus a Free plan.

| Tier | Subscription (billed annually) | Pay-per-event | Base attendee limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | 100 |
| Essentials | ~$99/mo | $299/event | 200 |
| Premium | ~$208/mo | $599/event | 1,000 |
| Ultimate | ~$416/mo | $1,499/event | 5,000 |

- **Attendee add-on:** $0.50 / attendee beyond the tier limit.
- Subscription tiers organize **unlimited events**. Monthly figures are annual-billing effective rates.
- *Source: zoho.com/backstage/pricing.html + Zoho help KB, corroborated by G2, Capterra, fgrade, SaaSworthy.*

#### Eventify ✅ Verified (cadence ⚠️)
Per-event / per-venue pricing, free trial, **no free plan**.

| Tier | Price | Attendee credits | Key adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Pro | $249 /event/venue | ~250 | Microsite, registration/ticketing, **badge designer, onsite badge printing, check-in** |
| Enterprise Pro (white-label) | $599 /event/venue | ~400 | Event/web app, 1-1 meetings, agenda, gamification, analytics |
| Enterprise (top) | up to $1,199.99 | ~1,500 | AI copilot/analytics, custom development |

- **Notable:** publishes **onsite badge printing + check-in at the entry ($249) tier** — unusual in the market.
- **Ambiguity:** some sources say "per year," most say per-event/per-venue; the $249 tier was seen temporarily promoted free.
- *Source: eventify.io/pricing + G2, Capterra, SaaSworthy.*

#### Accelevents ✅ Verified (tier $ = gap)
Two-part model.

| Plan group | Ticketing/platform fee | Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Free & Starter | **$1.50 + 3.5% per paid ticket** (free tickets exempt), auto-collected via organizer's Stripe/Square | low/none |
| Professional, Business, Enterprise, White Label | **$0 platform fee** — only third-party card processing (2.9% + $0.30, passable to buyers) | flat subscription (custom-quoted; exact $ not captured) |

- **White Label** adds branding removal, custom URL/email domain, branded mobile app.
- *Source: support.accelevents.com articles 6285214 / 6323728 + checkthat.ai 2026.*

### B) Engagement / networking mid-tier

#### Swapcard ✅ Verified
Two-product structure.

| Product | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Event Engagement Platform | **from $610 / year** (free tier up to ~250 attendees) | Full mobile/web app, AI networking, virtual events, hosted-buyer tools, exhibitor lead mgmt |
| Registration & Management Platform (end-to-end) | **Quote-only** | **Onsite badging**, exhibitor management, advanced reporting |

- Price scales with attendee/exhibitor volume. Onsite badging is behind the quote-only tier.
- *Source: swapcard.com/pricing + Capterra, G2, GetApp, SoftwareFinder.*

#### Whova ✅ Verified
- **No public tier table.** All-in-one platform sold as a **quote-only per-event fee** (not per-seat) — organizers submit event details for a price.
- **Paid-ticket fee:** 3.0% + $0.99 per paid ticket; **unlimited free tickets carry no ticketing fee.**
- Separate Stripe processing (2.9%/3.4% + $0.30). Optional add-ons (e.g. unlimited document uploads +$2,000).
- *Source: whova.com/pricing + LineUpr, GetApp, Capterra.*

#### Brella ✅ Verified
- **Quote-only, no free plan, no public starting price.** Two named plans:
  - **Professional** — single event, priced by attendee count + consulting services.
  - **Portfolio** — multiple events, priced by per-event attendee count + consulting + number of events, with volume/pre-pay discounts.
- *Source: brella.io/pricing + help.brella.io, SaaSworthy, SpotSaaS, Capterra.*

### D) Regional India / GCC — transaction-fee model

#### Townscript ✅ Verified
No monthly subscription, no setup cost; **free events are free.**

| Region | Townscript fee | Gateway | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| **India** | 2.99% (1.99% + ₹10) | + 1.99% | + 2% attendee platform fee |
| **International** | 1.99% + $2 per ticket | 2.9% domestic / 3.9% intl cards | — |

- "We earn when you earn." *Source: townscript.com/i/pricing + support docs.*

#### Explara / ExplaraX ✅ (free tier only) / paid tiers refuted
- **Free Starter (verified):** 1 event/month, up to 100 registrations, 1 WhatsApp community (~200 members), 5 digital products.
- **Paid-tier scaling and white-label per-registration pricing were REFUTED (1-2)** — do not rely on any specific paid figure. WhatsApp-first community/event product.
- *Source: go.explara.com / explarax.com/pricing.*

#### KonfHub ✅ Verified (transaction fee) / one-time plans refuted
- Free for free-ticket events; **pay-as-you-go per paid ticket**, no setup/annual fees.
- Standard **~1.75% per paid ticket**; **Lite Plan** fixed 2% + GST (2.36% total, India-only, UPI-only, up to 300 registrations then ₹10/ticket). 18% GST applies to KonfHub + gateway fee.
- **One-time $499/$1499 plans were REFUTED (1-2)** — subscription structure beyond transaction fees is uncertain.
- *Source: konfhub.com/pricing + blog, Capterra, GetApp.*

### E) Global ticketing — Eventbrite ✅ Verified (US pricing)
- **Publish unlimited free events of any size at no cost.** Old Flex plan discontinued; paid Pro plans ($15–$100/mo) only add email-marketing capacity — **not required** to publish or sell.
- **Paid tickets only:** Service Fee **3.7% + $1.79 per paid ticket** + Payment Processing **2.9% per paid order**, across all plans (US). Fee caps removed. Service fee passable to attendees or absorbed by organizer.
- *Geographic note:* UK/EU/India rates differ but were not captured this pass. *Source: eventbrite.com/organizer/pricing + SimpleTix, CheckoutPage, AnyRoad.*

## 5. Recommended pricing structure for EventHex

EventHex is an all-in-one event-management platform with strong India/GCC presence competing against Western SaaS incumbents. The positioning gap is clear: **transparent, mid-market pricing that bundles onsite badging/check-in — which competitors hide behind quote-only enterprise tiers — while offering the transaction-fee flexibility the India/GCC market expects.**

### Recommended: a hybrid 4-tier + transaction-fee model

| Tier | Target | Suggested price | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Trial / small free events | $0 | 1 event, up to ~100 registrations, registration + basic ticketing, mobile app, basic analytics. **Free events always free.** |
| **Starter** (per-event) | Small paid events / SMB | **$99–$149 / event** (or ~2% + fixed per paid ticket, whichever the organizer prefers) | Up to ~250 attendees, microsite, ticketing, check-in app, badge designer, coupons |
| **Professional** (per-event or annual) | Mid-market conferences | **$299–$399 / event** or ~$249/mo annual | Up to ~1,000 attendees, **onsite badge printing + hardware check-in**, networking/1-1 meetings, agenda, gamification, exhibitor/lead capture, full analytics |
| **Business / White-Label** | Agencies, franchises, large events | **$799–$1,199 / event** or custom annual | 1,500+ attendees, white-label + custom domain, branded app, AI copilot, API/integrations, priority support, multi-event portfolio |
| **Enterprise** | 5,000+ attendees / GCC govt & corporate | Quote | Unlimited scale, SLAs, dedicated onsite hardware, custom development, SSO |

### Why this positioning wins

1. **Publish onsite badging at the Professional tier**, not enterprise — directly undercuts Swapcard/Whova/Brella/Cvent (all quote-only) and matches only Eventify (which starts higher and per-venue).
2. **Offer both per-event and transaction-fee billing** so India/GCC organizers (used to Townscript/KonfHub's "free events free, pay per ticket") aren't forced into a Western subscription. A ~2% + fixed per-paid-ticket option undercuts Eventbrite (3.7% + $1.79) and Townscript (2.99% + ₹10) meaningfully.
3. **Keep entry pricing between the two worlds:** above the India transaction-fee floor but well below Western SaaS incumbents — e.g. Professional at ~$299/event vs Zoho $599/Eventify $599.
4. **Region-specific pricing (India/GCC in INR/AED, West in USD).** Every incumbent is USD-flat; localized pricing + free-events-free is a concrete regional advantage.
5. **Transparent, published pricing** as a brand differentiator — the majority of enterprise competitors (Cvent, Bizzabo, RainFocus, Whova, Brella) are quote-only, which mid-market buyers dislike.

## 6. Known gaps & open questions

- **No verified pricing** was obtained for **Cvent, Bizzabo, RingCentral/Hopin, Hubilo, Airmeet, Splash, RainFocus, Dreamcast** — enterprise players are known quote-only but figures here are indicative only. A targeted follow-up (or direct sales-quote requests) is needed for firm numbers.
- **Geographic variance** (US vs UK/EU vs India/GCC) was only firmly captured for **Townscript** (IN vs Intl) and **Eventbrite** (US). Whether Zoho/Eventify/Swapcard/Accelevents localize pricing for India/GCC is unconfirmed.
- **Refuted claims** (do not use): Explara paid-tier scaling, Explara white-label $0.99/registration + 4% gateway, KonfHub one-time $499/$1499 plans.
- **Exact per-tier subscription $** for Accelevents' Professional/Business/Enterprise were not captured.
- Every vendor page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch; verification relied on search-index snapshots + aggregators. High consensus, but not first-hand page reads. **Confirm any number before publishing it externally.**
